Driving License Controlled Smart Vehicle

Name of the Office: Institute of computers technology

Challenge: As per RTO rule, driving a vehicle without license is an offense and manually it is very difficult to check everyone who is not holding license and driving vehicle.

The Problem

Every year many accidents happen and people die in road accidents. More people have died in road accidents than in all the wars India has fought. And it is really scary to drive on the roads when nobody observes traffic rules.

150,000+

Road accident deaths annually in India

1,100+

Accidents occur every day on Indian roads

60%

Of accidents involve people aged 18-35 years

Access to the vehicle must be prevented for the person who is not holding driving license. Many awareness programs on road safety arranged for different age group especially for youths, but still we didn't find any remarkable positive result.

So there must be some system developed which can automatically prevent the access to the vehicle that is not eligible for driving and not bearing a license and thus cannot be accessible for a vehicle thief.

Proposed Solution

Our solution is a smart vehicle access control system that integrates with the existing driving license infrastructure to ensure only licensed drivers can operate vehicles.

🔐 Biometric Authentication

Integration of fingerprint or facial recognition with driving license database to verify driver identity before vehicle operation.

📱 Mobile App Integration

Smartphone-based verification system that connects to RTO databases for real-time license validation.

🚗 Vehicle Immobilizer

Electronic system that prevents engine start until valid license verification is completed.

Target Users

State Govt. RTO Office

Streamlined license verification and enforcement

Traffic Police

Enhanced road safety and easier law enforcement

Citizens

Safer roads and vehicle theft prevention

Expected Outcomes

  • Deaths due to Road accidents can be reduced by Preventing access to vehicle from a person without license
  • Prevent access to vehicles by individuals other than the actual registered user
  • Simplify the complexity of detecting persons without license by RTO
  • Enable RTO and Police to quickly and easily detect Identity of person met with an accident
  • Significant reduction in vehicle theft cases

Impact

State Govt. RTO office, Traffic Police & Citizens can benefitted with this system through:

  • Reduced road accidents and fatalities
  • More efficient law enforcement
  • Enhanced public safety
  • Decreased vehicle theft rates
  • Better compliance with traffic regulations

Driving License controlled smart vechicle

Problem Statement: Controlled Vehicle Act

As per the Road Traffic Act, driving without a license is an offence. Most people who are caught driving without a valid license are those who have just obtained their license or have had their license suspended or revoked. It is also true that some people drive the vehicle in an intoxicated state or are suffering from some disease which affects their driving.

Expanded Problem Statement

The challenge is to create a system that controls the vehicle if the driver does not have a valid license or is under the influence of alcohol or drugs, or is suffering from a medical condition that affects their driving. The system should notify the police and the nearest hospital and provide the identity of the person with the help of facial recognition.

Expected Outcomes

The system should be able to identify the driver using facial recognition and match the identity with the database of license holders. If the identity matches the criteria, the vehicle should be controlled (e.g., slowed down or stopped) and the authorities should be notified.

  • Integrate AI for real-time driver verification.
  • Ensure data privacy and compliance with Indian road laws.
  • Prototype using affordable hardware like Raspberry Pi or mobile apps.
